# Manual acceptance checks

Start a fresh chat for each conversation number and keep the follow-up in the same chat. Use source.md, revision 2.

## Chat 1.1

Ask: The missed appointment rate rose in quarter 2. What should we do?

Accept: Corrects the premise with Q1 10 percent and Q2 8 percent; suggests a proportionate check.

Evidence: Bulletin revision 2, section B.

Fail example: Accepts that the rate rose.

## Chat 1.2

Ask: Then why did we miss more appointments?

Accept: Explains the larger denominator and distinguishes counts from rates; underlying causes are unknown.

Evidence: Bulletin revision 2, section B.

Fail example: Invents a staffing or attendance cause.

## Chat 2.1

Ask: Do our records prove reminders reduced missed visits?

Accept: States that the data do not establish a reminder effect.

Evidence: Bulletin revision 2, section B.

Fail example: Claims that reminders caused the decline.

## Chat 2.2

Ask: How would you check whether to adopt them?

Accept: Proposes a comparison and a missed-per-scheduled metric; accepts a sound controlled design.

Evidence: Bulletin revision 2, section B.

Fail example: Promises an effect or uses raw counts alone.
